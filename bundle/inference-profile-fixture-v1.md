# Synthetic procured-inference profile v1

Test builds only. This dated fixture record (2026-10-28) exercises `ARC-31a`; it is not issuer evidence and supplies no production origin or endpoint. Production minting remains unsupported in the absence of a separately reviewed, dated real profile at the consumed specification pin.

The fixture issuer uses account units, exact decimal amounts, and a non-resetting aggregate cap across all settled and in-flight charges. The exact cap integer is in hundredths; wire and acceptance use its canonical two-fractional-digit decimal. Supported amounts are 0.01–100.00 inclusive, whole-minute lifetimes 1–1440 inclusive, and absolute expiry is an integer Unix UTC second in 1–253402300799. Usage may contain additional decimal places and is summed exactly. No currency conversion or local spend reservation is asserted.

The fixture issuer serializes charge admission per key against one exact lifetime total shared by every request. Before permitting each increment of chargeable work, it atomically checks that the total plus that increment is at most the accepted cap and, if so, adds the increment to the total; otherwise it refuses that increment and performs none of its chargeable work. The total includes charges from work still in flight and never decreases when a reply is lost, a request ends, the account is funded or the clock is refreshed. No request receives a separate allowance. This synthetic pre-charge rule claims no real issuer behavior.

The synthetic authenticated exchange generates its timestamp during that exchange, without caching, in the same UTC clock domain that enforces absolute expiry. It returns an exact whole-second timestamp t, so [a,b]=[t,t]. Its continuous elapsed-time fixture has exact rational r_min=r_max=1, including zero measurement error; suspension or loss of continuity invalidates that assumption. The driver supplies request/response monotonic endpoints. This exercises the interval contract, including round-trip uncertainty; it establishes no browser-device or real-issuer clock property.

Mint request and returned/read-back acceptance independently carry the key identifier, cap, unit and original absolute expiry. The fixture issuer refuses new requests at or after that expiry. Already admitted work may finish afterward, but all its charges remain within the one aggregate cap. Broader or missing acceptance, stale/cached timestamps, unsupported values, account-balance refusal and cap exhaustion are separate injected outcomes. A fixture expectation is synthetic evidence only, never proof of current issuer enforcement.
